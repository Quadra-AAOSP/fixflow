FixFlow Technician Participation Agreement

Version test-v1 · Published for acceptance during technician registration and work access.

Participation and site contracts

Technician registration submits verification details, structured skills, and records acceptance of this agreement. You may request a site contract after signup. Site approval, a recorded contract, and site vetting are still required before contracted work access is granted. Registration does not guarantee assignments or income.

Identity and qualifications

Provide accurate contact details, relevant skills, experience and current qualifications. Supply licence or insurance evidence through an approved secure channel when required for the work. Update expired credentials and disclose relevant restrictions before accepting work.

Work scope and safety

Accept only work you are qualified and authorized to perform. Agree the scope, access arrangements, hazards, permits and emergency contact with the site before starting. Stop unsafe work and notify the responsible site contact. Use the appropriate protective equipment and follow applicable safety requirements.

Fees and responsibilities

Record pricing, materials, expenses, payment timing, insurance, warranties, liability and cancellation arrangements in a separate site or job agreement before work begins. Platform participation alone does not establish these commercial terms or determine employment status.

Report records and conduct

Keep work records accurate and relevant. Do not claim completion without performing the agreed work. Communicate respectfully, disclose conflicts of interest and use authorized workflows for assignments and status changes. Report errors or suspected misuse to the site administrator.

Privacy and photographs

Access report information only for authorized maintenance work. Upload only necessary photographs you are permitted to share; avoid faces, identity documents, access codes and unrelated personal information. Do not redistribute reports or signed photo links. Use AI assistance only after reviewing its disclosure and removing confidential or identifying information.

Account security and leaving a site

Keep your credentials private and report suspected compromise promptly. Access may change when a site relationship ends. Removing a platform contract record changes access; it does not by itself settle outstanding invoices, terminate a separate legal agreement or erase records subject to a retention obligation.

Changes and acceptance

Review the published version before accepting. The service records acceptance against its configured version and document URL. Reading this document does not record acceptance, grant access or replace a site contract. Material changes should be published as a new version for review.

Items to finalize before publication

The platform owner must confirm the contracting entity, support and privacy contacts, applicable law and dispute process, retention schedule, insurance requirements and any limits of liability. The English and Chinese texts require review together, including which language governs if they differ.
